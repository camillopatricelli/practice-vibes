Who it's for

Students practising alone

Practice time is logged automatically while you play: only the minutes you actually play count. A rotation of topics shows what you've been neglecting, and a session planner splits your time for you.

Teachers

Send a lesson as one file: notes, songs, links and an assignment with a target tempo. Students see it on their Today screen and can send back takes and a summary of their practice.

Musicians who want one tool

Looper with drum machine, tuner, stem separation, your own plugin rig and a MIDI footswitch, set up once and always ready.

What it does

  • Song player: audio files and YouTube videos, speed 25–150 % without changing the pitch, pitch ±12 semitones, A-B loop, waveform zoom.
  • Stems (Windows, Mac): mute the guitar, bass or vocals on the record and play the part yourself.
  • Chords, beats and time signatures recognised automatically and shown as the song plays.
  • Metronome with subdivisions, swing, a speed trainer, and clicks that follow the song.
  • 4-track looper locked to the beat, with overdubs, latency calibration and a drum machine.
  • Tuner: needle or strobe, alternate tunings, check all strings in one strum.
  • Takes: record yourself over the song and listen back in step.
  • Topics: your lessons, songs, PDFs and links in a folder you choose; share it across devices via iCloud Drive.
  • Rig: your own effect and instrument plugins (AUv3, VST3, Audio Units).
  • Footswitch: any MIDI pedal, USB or Bluetooth, to run the looper and more hands-free.

iPad

iPadOS 16 or later

Not on the App Store: installed with the free SideStore tool and your Apple ID, refreshed every 7 days. You need a computer once.

How to install

Mac

macOS 12 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel

Not tested on a real Mac yet. The app isn't notarised, so macOS asks you to allow it once.

Free, and staying free

Practice Vibes is free software under the GNU AGPLv3. No price, no account, no ads, no in-app purchases. The app sends nothing about you anywhere: your library and practice log stay on your devices, or in your own cloud folder if you choose one. Details in the privacy statement.
