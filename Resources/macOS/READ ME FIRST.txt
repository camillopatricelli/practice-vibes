Practice Vibes for macOS (test build)
=====================================

Free software under the GNU AGPLv3 (see LICENSE.txt and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

This build is not notarised by Apple (no paid developer account), so macOS will
warn the first time you open it. That is expected.

Install
-------
1. Drag "Practice Vibes" onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Applications and try to open Practice Vibes once. macOS says it can't
   check the app for malicious software: click Done / Cancel.
3. Allow it:
   - macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later: System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
     down to "Practice Vibes was blocked..." and click "Open Anyway", then
     confirm with your password.
   - macOS 12 to 14: right-click (or Control-click) the app > Open > Open.
   - Or, in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Practice Vibes.app"
4. When asked, allow microphone access: it is needed for your guitar input.
   (After installing a new build macOS may ask again.)

Plugins
-------
Audio Units are listed automatically. For VST3 plugins, open the Rig tab and
click "Scan VST3" once.

Bluetooth MIDI footswitch
-------------------------
Use the Bluetooth button in the top bar, or pair it in
Audio MIDI Setup > Window > Show MIDI Studio > Bluetooth.

Problems?
---------
The log file is here (Finder > Go > Go to Folder... and paste the path):
  ~/Library/Application Support/Practice Vibes/PracticeVibes.log
Please open an issue at https://github.com/camillopatricelli/practice-vibes/issues
with a short description of what happened, the log, and the text from
Audio settings > About Practice Vibes > Copy diagnostic info.
