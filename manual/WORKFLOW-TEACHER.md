# For teachers

The lesson goes home with the student as one file, their practice logs itself, and what they send back lands where
you can comment on it. No server, no accounts: topics travel as files, by AirDrop, Mail or a chat app.

For your own practice, [WORKFLOW-STUDENT.md](WORKFLOW-STUDENT.md) applies too.

---

## Once: turn on I teach

**Audio settings** › **Display** › **I teach**. You get a **Teaching** button in the top bar, **Assignment...** on
each topic, a **Student** box, the **Students** and groups lists in the **Tags** menu, and teacher options in
**Share...**. Students never see these.

---

## Prepare a lesson (once per lesson, reusable)

1. **A topic for the lesson** ("Lesson 12 – modes"): backing track, PDF, YouTube example, and what to practise in
   **Notes**. **Private notes** are for you only: they never leave your device.
2. **Marks** on the song: a saved loop for each passage, with its speed and a short comment ("bar 17: watch the
   fingering").
3. **Assignment...**: the goal, a target tempo, minutes a day, the date of the next lesson.

Keep good lesson topics as templates: the same topic can go to every student who needs it.

---

## Teach

Tap **Teaching** when the lesson starts. The app works as usual (play, slow down, loop, record a demo) but writes
nothing to your practice log. Takes are named "Demo ...". When you tap **Teaching** again, the app offers to send the
topic. (The app always starts with Teaching off.)

---

## Send it

**Share...** on the topic:
- Type the student's first name or nickname in **Student** (stays on your devices, never sent).
- Tick the files to include: your recordings and documents are ticked, songs aren't (students use their own copy).
  The dialog shows if it fits in an email. Add your name so they see who it's from.
- **Several students**: **As a new topic** gives each one their own copy.
- **An ensemble**: **Tags** › **New group (ensemble)...**, add the members, pick the group under **To**: one file for
  the group chat, or one per member.
- **Someone without the app**: **Send as** › **Document for anyone**, a web page with the notes, links and files that
  opens on any phone or computer.

**Links**: the **Links** tab is your list of useful websites (a circle of fifths explainer, a scales site). Add them
to a lesson topic with **Add link...** so they travel with it.

---

## Follow the student's week

- The student opens the file: the topic goes into their rotation and your assignment shows on their **Today** tab,
  filled in by their automatic log ("Teacher: 5 / 15 min today, target 96 bpm (last 88)").
- Before the next lesson they record a take and send the topic back with **Share...**. If they tick **Include my
  practice on this topic**, you get the totals (time per week, tempo, days in a row), nothing else from their log.
- Open the returned file: the preview shows who sent it and the practice summary. Add comments on their take at the
  right moment with **Marks** ("1:23 rushing here").
- For a group, each member's reply becomes their own topic, and the topic you sent shows who has replied.

## Reply with the next version

Change the notes and the assignment (new target, next date) and **Share...** again. Their copy updates in place;
their private notes stay as they are.

---

## Habits that pay off

- **Teaching on for every lesson**, so your History shows only your own practice.
- **A realistic assignment** (minutes a day, target tempo): it becomes the student's daily goal.
- **Comment on takes rather than writing long notes**: a mark at 1:23 is clearer than a paragraph.
- **The Students list** (Tags menu) shows one student's topics at a glance.
