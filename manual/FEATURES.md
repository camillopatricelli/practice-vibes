# Practice Vibes – features

Practice Vibes is a free practice app (free software under the GNU AGPLv3) for guitarists, bassists and other musicians. It puts the things you'd normally need several apps
for in one place: a song player that slows songs down and loops them, chord recognition, a metronome, a looper with a
drum machine, a tuner, your effect plugins, a footswitch, and a place for your lessons and practice notes. It also keeps
track of how much you really play.

It runs on **iPad**, **Windows** and **macOS**.

---

## Today: your practice at a glance
- **The app records, you play.** Practice time is logged automatically from your instrument input: only the minutes you
  actually play count, silences don't. With a microphone instead of an audio interface (it hears the room too), the
  time counts between **Start** and **Pause**; the app tells the two apart by itself, and you can change it per input.
  Notes played on a **MIDI keyboard** count as playing too (USB or Bluetooth MIDI), with or without an audio input;
  a keyboard next to a room microphone can be timed from its notes only.
- Per topic, the log also keeps the tempo or song speed you reached, whether you used the looper, and an optional note. It does so autonomously. You may edit data if you want to, but you won't need to. Focus on your playing, the app does the boring work.
- **Your teacher's assignment** shows on the Today tab: "Teacher: 5 / 15 min today, target 96 bpm (last 88), lesson
  Fri 20 Nov". A topic received with an assignment goes straight into your practice rotation.
- **"Practise"** on a topic brings back the setup you left it with (song, loop, speed, stem mix, metronome tempo).
- **"Haven't practised since…"** shows what you've been neglecting; **Last two weeks** shows what you did.
- **Roll the dice**: picks a topic for you, favouring the ones you've neglected. You can narrow it down by tag.
- **Plan a session**: "I have 45 minutes" → 3 to 5 slots of topics. Re-roll a slot, change the minutes, then Start.
  The slot clock only runs while you play, and a soft chime tells you when time's up. Nothing moves on by itself.
- **Saved plans**: keep a session you like ("Morning warm-up") and start it again later, as it is or tweaked for today.
- **History**: minutes per week, time per topic, tempo or speed progress over time, every session with its notes.
- **Practice without playing** (ear training, theory): a topic can be timed by the clock, or you add the time by hand.
- Wrong entry? Tap it to correct or delete it, or move it to another topic (free practice can get a topic afterwards).
- No streaks, no reminders, no notifications.

## Practice: the song player
- Plays WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 and M4A files.
- **Speed** 25–150 % without changing the pitch; **pitch** ±12 semitones without changing the speed.
- **A-B loop**: drag across the waveform, or use Set A / Set B. **Snap** the loop to bars or beats.
- **Waveform zoom** (pinch, or Ctrl/Cmd + mouse wheel), with an overview strip, for precise loop points.
- **YouTube videos** play inside the app with the same timeline, A-B loop, speed and volume. A compact player floats
  over the other tabs.
- **Stems**: split a song into vocals, guitar, bass, drums, piano and other, then mute, solo or rebalance them.
  Speed, pitch and loops keep working. Separation takes about 3 minutes for a 4-minute song (Windows and Mac).
- **Record a take** (top-bar Rec, or a footswitch): records your instrument after the effects, saved in the topic you're
  practising. Replays in step with the song, at the speed and loop you played it at. **Export mix** makes one .m4a
  file (song + your take) to send to a teacher.

## Chords, beats and time signatures
- Chords are recognised automatically, including slash chords (e.g. D/F#), and shown both as a big chord display and
  as a lane above the waveform. The chord card also shows the key, tempo and time signature, the next chord with a
  countdown, and the chords coming up.
- Beats, bars and time signature are detected too (3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 7/4, including changes inside a song).

## Metronome
- Tempo, beats per bar, accented downbeat.
- Subdivisions (8ths, triplets, 16ths) with their own volume; **swing** from straight to shuffle to dotted.
- Sounds: beep, click or drums.
- **Speed trainer**: e.g. add 2 bpm every 4 bars (or every minute) up to a target tempo.
- **Follow song beats**: the clicks follow the song's detected beats, also when it's slowed down or looped.
- Settings are kept between sessions.

## Looper and drum machine
- **4-track looper** locked to the metronome's bars: fixed length (1–16 bars) or free, overdub with undo, mute, clear.
- Record your instrument **after the effects or before** them.
- **Latency compensation** with a calibration helper (play short muted notes on the clicks), so overdubs land on time.
- Loops keep playing while you switch to other tabs. Save and reload loop sessions (one WAV per track plus a mix).
- **Built-in drum machine**: acoustic (sampled) or electronic kit, about a dozen starter patterns, a simple step
  editor (8ths / triplets / 16ths, swing, accents, ghost notes), 1–8 bar patterns that restart with your loops,
  your own patterns saved by name.

## Tuner
- Chromatic tuner with needle or **strobe** view; reference A4 430–450 Hz.
- **Guitar**, **bass** (4, 5 or 6 strings) or **other** (plain chromatic, for any instrument or voice).
- **Alternate tunings**: 10 guitar presets (Drop D, DADGAD, Open G, half-step down…), 7 bass presets (incl. 5-string
  low B), plus your own.
- **Strum all strings**: strum the open strings once and see every string's tuning at the same time.
- Mutes your instrument while tuning if you want. Open it from the top bar or a footswitch.

## Topics and Links
- **Topics** hold your lessons: title, tags, notes, and a list of resources (songs, PDFs, images, videos, links).
  Songs open in the player, documents in a built-in viewer.
- Everything lives in a normal **library folder** you choose. Put it in iCloud Drive (or another cloud folder on a
  computer) to have the same library, practice log, plans, takes and stems on all your devices.
- Search, tag filter, an **Inbox** for loose files; songs opened from elsewhere can be added to the Inbox.
- **Links**: a separate list of bookmarks to useful websites (a circle of fifths explainer, a scales site…). Websites
  open in your browser, YouTube links in the app's player.
- **Storage and clean-up**: see what uses space (takes, stems, loops…) and remove old items.
- **Share a topic** with a teacher, a student or a bandmate as one file (AirDrop, Mail, WhatsApp…): you choose which
  files go in, and see if it fits in an email. Opening it in the app adds the topic, or updates it if you already have
  it; songs left out are found in your own library. **Private notes** never leave your device. You can add your
  name, so the person receiving it sees who it's from.
- **Document for anyone**: the same topic as a web page with the notes, links and files, for people who don't have the
  app (opens on any phone or computer, prints to PDF).
- **Teaching mode** ("I teach" setting): a Teaching switch in the top bar; during a lesson the app works as usual but
  nothing goes to your practice log. The app always starts with it off. When the lesson ends the app offers to send the topic; takes recorded during
  the lesson are named "Demo".
- **Marks**: markers and saved loops (with their speed and pitch) on a song, each with a short comment ("bar 17: watch
  the fingering"), shown on the waveform; comments at a time on a recorded take. They travel with the topic.
- **For teachers**: an assignment per topic (goal, target tempo, minutes a day, next lesson) shown on the student's
  Today tab; topics grouped by student (first name or nickname only, never sent); the same lesson sent to several
  students as separate copies; **groups** (an ensemble gets one topic in one go: one file for the group chat or one per
  member; each member's reply becomes their own topic, and the topic shows who sent something back); "sent / received"
  dates. Students can send back their takes and, if they choose, a
  summary of their practice on the topic (totals only).

## Rig (effects)
- Instrument input → a chain of your own effect plugins → output, with input gain, output level and meters.
- **AUv3** plugins on iPad, **VST3** on Windows, **Audio Units and VST3** on macOS, each with its own interface.
- Add, reorder, bypass and remove plugins. The chain and every plugin's settings are saved and restored.
- **Instrument plugins** (piano, synth, drum sampler) played from a **MIDI keyboard**: the sound goes through your
  effects and into loops and takes.
- **Scenes**: up to four saved sounds of the same chain (say Clean, Crunch, Lead), switched with one tap or a
  footswitch while you play. A scene keeps every plugin's settings, which plugins are on, and the output level.

## Footswitch
- Any **MIDI footswitch**, over USB or **Bluetooth MIDI** (pairing built into the app).
- Up to four switches, each "learned" by pressing it, with a **tap** and a **hold** action: looper track record /
  overdub / play, clear, mute, undo, mute or clear all, start / stop metronome, tuner, bypass a plugin, record a take,
  mute one of the song's stems (e.g. the guitar, bass or piano), switch rig scenes.
- **Two modes**: Looper mode (switches A–D run the looper) and Scene mode (switches A–D pick scenes 1–4), each with
  its own actions. Hold D to switch between them (tap D on a latching pedal).

## Look
- Dark and light themes, or follow the system setting.
- Language: English or Italian; follows the device unless you pick one in Settings.
- **About** (Audio settings › About Practice Vibes): version and build number, platform, developer and contact,
  links to the guide, privacy statement, licence and source code, the open source licences, and **Copy diagnostic
  info** (version, build, device and audio setup, to paste into a bug report).

## Known limits
- **iPad**: only iCloud Drive works for a shared library (Google Drive and others can't be chosen as a folder on the
  iPad).
- **macOS**: not tested on a real Mac yet.
- Chord recognition also "finds" chords in speech or drum-only tracks.
- Recording over a YouTube video: the take plays back on its own, not in step with the video.
- Windows: the app isn't code-signed, so Windows asks once before opening a new version (see the Install chapter).
