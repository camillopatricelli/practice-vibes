# For students practising alone

You want to practise regularly, on the right things, and see that it's working. Practice Vibes keeps the diary for
you, decides what's next when you can't, and remembers how you left each piece.

**Before you start:** an audio interface or a MIDI keyboard makes the practice log fully automatic. See
[AUDIO-SETUP.md](AUDIO-SETUP.md).

---

## Once per thing you're learning (5 minutes)

1. **Make a topic.** **Topics** › **+ New topic**: one topic per goal ("Autumn Leaves – solo", "Alternate picking",
   "Lesson 12"). Add a tag or two (#technique, #songs).
2. **Put everything in it.** **Add files...** for the backing track and the PDF, **Add link...** for the YouTube
   lesson. Write what to practise in **Notes** ("bars 9–16, aim for 120 bpm").
3. **Turn on In practice rotation.** The topic now shows on the **Today** tab.

Got the lesson from your teacher as a topic file? Open it: steps 1–3 are done, and the teacher's assignment shows on
Today ("Teacher: 5 / 15 min today, target 96 bpm").

---

## Every session

1. **Today tab.** The rotation lists your topics, **longest neglected first**. Pick one, or **Roll the dice** and let
   the app choose (it favours what you've been avoiding; the tag box narrows it to e.g. #technique).
2. **Practise.** The song or video opens. The first time, set it up:
   - **Loop** the hard part: drag across the waveform (zoom in, **Snap** to bars for clean loop points).
   - **Slow it down**: **Speed** 70 %, then raise it step by step.
   - **Metronome**: tempo, subdivisions, or **Follow song beats** to click along with the song. For exercises, the
     **speed trainer** raises the tempo for you (e.g. +2 bpm every 4 bars up to a target).
3. **Play.** That's all. The app logs the minutes you actually play (pauses after about 5 seconds of silence), the
   speed or tempo you played at, and whether you used the looper. Next time, **Practise** brings back the song, loop,
   speed and tempo exactly as you left them.

Want to hear yourself? Top-bar **Rec** records a take into the topic; it plays back in step with the song.

---

## More time? Plan a session

**Plan a session...** › "I have 45 minutes": the app suggests 3 to 5 slots of rotation topics. Re-roll or change
them, then **Start**. The slot clock only runs while you play; a soft chime says when time's up, and nothing moves on
until you choose **Next topic**.

Like the routine? **Save as plan...** ("Morning warm-up") and start it again any day from **Saved plans**. A slot set
to **Random** picks a different neglected topic each time.

---

## Every week or two: look back

**History** shows minutes per week, time per topic and, per topic, the **speed or tempo line** going up. **Last two
weeks** on Today is the quick view. A take from three weeks ago next to today's is the best motivation there is.

Wrong entry, or time that went to Free practice? Tap it to fix it. Practised away from the app? **+ Add time...**.

---

## Habits that pay off

- **Always practise through a topic** (Practise, or open one of its files). Otherwise the time goes to Free practice.
- **Raise the speed in steps** (70 → 80 → 90 %): each step becomes a point on your progress line.
- **Keep the rotation short** (5–10 topics). Take finished ones out; they keep their history.
- **Record a take once a week** per topic: the before/after you'll want later.
- **Library in iCloud Drive** if you use more than one device: same topics and log everywhere (guide chapter 9).
