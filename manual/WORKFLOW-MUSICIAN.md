# For musicians who want one tool

Looper, drum machine, tuner, stems, your effect plugins and a footswitch, in one app that's set up once and remembers
everything. The practice log runs in the background whether you care about it or not.

**Before you start:** an audio interface and wired headphones. See [AUDIO-SETUP.md](AUDIO-SETUP.md).

---

## Set up your sound once: the Rig

**Rig** tab: your instrument → **Input gain** → your effect plugins → **Output level** → headphones.
- Plugins: **AUv3** on iPad (from the App Store), **VST3** on Windows (**Scan VST3** once), **Audio Units and VST3** on
  Mac. **Add to chain**, reorder with the arrows, **Bypass** like a pedal, **Open UI** for the plugin's own knobs.
- Turn on **Monitor input** (on headphones) to hear yourself.
- MIDI keyboard? Add an **[Instrument]** plugin (piano, synth) at the top of the chain and play it through your effects.

The chain and every plugin's settings come back next time. Details: guide chapter **10. Rig**.

## Tune

Top-bar **Tuner**: needle or **strobe**, **Guitar**, **Bass** (4, 5 or 6 strings) or **Other**. Alternate tunings
(Drop D, DADGAD, Open G, half-step down, 5-string low B...) or your own. **Strum all strings** once and see every
string at the same time. Details: chapter **8. Tuner**.

## Jam: looper and drums

1. **Looper** tab: set the **tempo** and **beats per bar** first (loops lock to the metronome's bars, so they never
   drift).
2. **Drums**: pick a starter pattern (Rock, Funk, Shuffle, Slow blues, Jazz swing, Bossa nova, 3/4, 6/8...), choose
   the **Acoustic** or **Electronic kit**, **Play drums**. **Mute clicks** keeps time without the metronome sound.
   **Edit pattern** for your own grooves.
3. **Record** on track 1: recording starts on the next bar line. With **Length** 1–16 bars it stops by itself; with
   **Free** tap again. Tap to **overdub**, **Undo overdub** if it went wrong. Up to 4 tracks.
4. **Record: After FX** (default) keeps the sound you looped with; **Before FX** lets you change the effects on loops
   afterwards.
5. **Calibrate...** once (12 short muted notes on the clicks), so overdubs land on time.
6. **Save loops** keeps the session (one WAV per track plus a mix, tempo and drum pattern).

Details: chapter **7. Looper and drum machine**.

## Play along with songs: stems

Open a song on the **Practice** tab, then split it into **stems** (vocals, guitar, bass, drums, piano, other) on
Windows or Mac: about 3 minutes for a 4-minute song. Mute the part you play and play it yourself, at any speed or
pitch. Stems saved in a shared library play on the iPad too. Chords and beats are detected for you.

Details: chapter **4. Player**.

## Hands-free: a MIDI footswitch

Any MIDI pedal, USB or **Bluetooth MIDI** (top-bar **Bluetooth** button). **Footswitch setup...** › **Learn A-D in a
row**, then give each switch a **Tap** and a **Hold** action:
- looper tracks (record / overdub / play, clear, mute), undo, mute or clear all,
- start / stop metronome, tuner on / off, record a take,
- mute a song's stem (e.g. the guitar), bypass a plugin.

Tap and hold need a **momentary** pedal (most can be set that way). Details: chapter **11. Footswitch**.

---

## Habits that pay off

- **Save the rig you like**: it's restored every time, plugins and settings included.
- **Footswitch on the looper**: tap to record, hold to clear, without letting go of your instrument.
- **Turn on monitoring only on headphones** (or leave it off if you hear yourself through an amp).
