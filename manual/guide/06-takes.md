# 6. Recording takes

Record yourself playing along, then listen back. It's the quickest way to hear what you really sound like, and to
compare this week with last month.

---

## Recording

1. Load the song (or not: you can record on your own) and get ready.
2. Tap **Rec** (red dot) in the top bar. It turns into a red **Stop m:ss** button counting the time.
3. Play. Start and stop the song, loop, jump around as you like: the app remembers what the song did.
4. Tap **Stop**. After "Saving..." the button says **Take saved**.

What's recorded: **your instrument only**, as you hear it (after the effects of your Rig). The song isn't mixed in;
it's lined up with your take when you replay it.

- The take is saved **in the topic you're practising**, as a resource marked TAKE. In free practice it goes to the
  **Inbox**.
- Takes shorter than **10 seconds** aren't kept ("Too short: < 10 s"), so a false start doesn't leave a file behind.
- Takes are .m4a files, about 1 MB per minute.
- A footswitch can start and stop recording (chapter 11), so your hands stay on your instrument.
- Takes recorded in teaching mode are named "Demo ..." (chapter 9).
- With a take open, **Marks** adds a comment at a time in the take ("1:23 rushing here"), for example when a teacher
  listens to a student's take.

---

## Listening back

Open a take from its topic (or from the topic's **History**, where takes are listed with the sessions). The Practice tab
shows "Take: … over <song>" and a take bar.

- **Play take**: your playing and the song play together, **in step**, exactly as when you recorded: same position,
  speed, pitch and loop. If you paused the song while recording, it's silent there while your playing goes on.
- Tap or drag the **take bar** to jump.
- **Song** and **Take** volume sliders set the balance (turn the song down to hear your timing more clearly).
- **Close take** gives you back your own speed, pitch and loop.

The app compensates for the delay of your audio setup (latency, chapter 2), so your playing doesn't sound late against
the song.

> Recorded over a **YouTube** video, or with no song: the take plays back on its own, without the video.

---

## Sharing a take

**Export mix...** (while a take is open) makes one .m4a file with the song and your playing together, balanced like
the volume sliders. Send it to your teacher or a friend.
- **Windows / Mac**: you choose where to save it.
- **iPad**: the share sheet opens (AirDrop, Mail, Files, Messages…).

If you used stems, the export uses the stem mix you hear.

---

## Tidying up

Remove a take from its topic (the resource's **…** menu › **Remove**). To clear out many at once, e.g. "takes older
than a month", use **Storage and clean-up** (chapter 9).
