# 13. Fretboard

The **Fretboard** tab is a chord chart for guitar: the seven chords of a key, drawn on the neck in every position.
It works offline. The page's own text is in English for now.

---

## Choosing what to see

- **Scale**: the major scale, its modes (Dorian, Mixolydian…), harmonic minor or melodic minor.
- **Key**: pick one to see real fret numbers and note names. With **No key**, frets are relative: **n** is the fret of
  the scale's root on the low E string.
- **Show**: **Arpeggios** (every chord tone in the position), **Triads** (three strings) or **7th chords** (drop 2 and
  drop 3 voicings).
- **Position**: one of the five CAGED shapes, one of the seven 3-notes-per-string patterns, or **Neck** to see every
  voicing of a chord on one string set, up the whole neck. You can also tap a shape on the neck map at the top.
- The row of chord names under the neck map (I, ii, iii… and **Scale**) picks the chord drawn large.

Colours: red is the root, blue the third, amber the fifth, violet the seventh.

---

## Practising with it

- **Play** sounds the chord shown; **All seven** plays them one after another.
- **Voice leading**: pick a progression (ii–V–I and others) to see each chord voiced close to the one before, with
  held notes ringed.
- **Quiz**: name the chord, its inversion, or the job of a ringed note. **Hear it** plays the voicing.

The page remembers your choices between sessions.

**On Windows:** the tab needs Microsoft WebView2, which comes with Windows 11 and up-to-date Windows 10. If it's
missing, the tab says so and offers **Get WebView2**.

**Sound:** Play uses the computer's or iPad's normal sound output, not the audio device chosen in the app's audio
settings. With an audio interface in exclusive mode (ASIO), you may hear nothing.
